# RAAHAT Data Sources

This document lists and details every PDF and dataset used in the RAAHAT vector database knowledge base.

---

## 1. Acceptance and Commitment Therapy Workbook
* **Filename:** `Acceptance_and_Commitment_Therapy_Workbook.pdf`
* **Purpose:** Provides Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) structured exercises, metaphors, and mindfulness techniques for emotional regulation and psychological flexibility.
* **Source:** Educational ACT worksheet collection.
* **License status:** License status not yet verified.

## 2. CBT Group Program for Depression: Patient Manual
* **Filename:** `CBT-Group-Program-for-Depression-Patient-Manual.pdf`
* **Purpose:** Core patient manual explaining Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) skills covering cognitive reframing, behavioral activation, and depression management.
* **Source:** University of Michigan Medicine (Department of Psychiatry).
* **License status:** License status not yet verified.

## 3. DBT Crisis Survival Skills Workbook
* **Filename:** `DBT-Crisis-Survival-Skills-Workbook.pdf`
* **Purpose:** Outlines Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) distress tolerance skills (STOP, TIPP, ACCEPTS, self-soothing) for managing acute crisis states.
* **Source:** Educational clinical training materials for DBT distress tolerance.
* **License status:** License status not yet verified.

## 4. Definitions and Examples of 15 Cognitive Distortions
* **Filename:** `Definitions and Examples of 15 Cognitive Distortions(1).pdf`
* **Purpose:** Defines and illustrates the 15 common cognitive distortions (e.g., catastrophizing, overgeneralization, mental filtering) to assist in reframing negative thoughts.
* **Source:** Cognitive Behavioral Therapy training handouts.
* **License status:** License status not yet verified.

## 5. Grounding Exercise Guide
* **Filename:** `Grounding-Exercise.pdf`
* **Purpose:** Step-by-step guide to physical and mental grounding exercises (such as the 5-4-3-2-1 sensory technique) to de-escalate anxiety and panic attacks.
* **Source:** Public clinical worksheet distribution.
* **License status:** License status not yet verified.

## 6. A Short Introduction to Psychological First Aid
* **Filename:** `PFA-Intro-low.pdf`
* **Purpose:** Reference for Psychological First Aid (PFA) principles (Look, Listen, Link) for providing immediate, non-intrusive support during crises.
* **Source:** International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (IFRC) Reference Centre for Psychosocial Support.
* **License status:** License status not yet verified.

## 7. Doing What Matters in Times of Stress: An Illustrated Guide
* **Filename:** `who_doing_what_matters_in_times_of_stress.pdf`
* **Purpose:** An illustrated guide to stress management, utilizing Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) coping skills.
* **Source:** World Health Organization (WHO).
* **License status:** Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 IGO (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO) license.
